# EditSharp

EditSharp is a node-based video editor. Everything it does can also be done without its windows:
from an extension running inside it, from a script, or from another program talking to it.

- [**The API**](articles/api.md): the services, commands and events, and how they fit together.
- [**Extensions**](articles/extensions.md): adding commands, views, menu items, settings pages and importers.
- [**Remote API**](articles/remote.md): driving a running app over JSON-RPC, with or without windows.
- [**Command catalogue**](articles/commands.md): every command, its keys and its arguments.
- [**API reference**](api/index.md): every public type, from the source's documentation.
